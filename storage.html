📦 Storage Management

Your phone storage is where all your apps, photos, videos, and files are saved. When your storage is full, your phone becomes slow and may stop working properly.

🗑️ Delete Unnecessary Files

Remove old photos, videos, downloads, and duplicate files to free space.

📱 Uninstall Unused Apps

Delete apps you don’t use anymore to gain more storage and improve speed.

🧹 Use Cleaning Apps

Use apps like Files by Google to remove junk files and cache safely.

☁️ Move to Cloud or SD Card

Store your files online or on an SD card to free internal storage.
