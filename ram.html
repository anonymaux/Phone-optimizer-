⚡ RAM Optimization

RAM (Random Access Memory) is responsible for running your apps smoothly. When too many apps are open, your phone becomes slow and less responsive.

📴 Close Background Apps

Always close apps you are not using to free up memory.

❌ Remove Unused Apps

Uninstall apps that you don’t use to reduce RAM usage.

⚙️ Disable Animations

Turn off animations in developer options to make your phone faster.

🔄 Restart Regularly

Restart your phone to refresh RAM and improve performance.
