🌐 Improve Internet Speed

A slow internet connection can make browsing, streaming, and downloading frustrating. Follow these tips to boost your phone’s connection.

✈️ Toggle Airplane Mode

Turn airplane mode on and off to reset your network connection quickly.

🌍 Change DNS

Use faster DNS services like Google DNS or Cloudflare to improve internet speed.

📶 Stay Close to Wi-Fi

Being near the Wi-Fi router improves signal strength and speed.

📱 Close Heavy Apps

Apps consuming a lot of data in the background can slow down your connection.
